Privacy Policy

Pedro: Louisiana Card Game · Effective October 8, 2026

Pedro is a single-player card game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Pedro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, Pedro saves a few things locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your game statistics (games played, wins, bids).
  • A game in progress, so you can resume it later.
  • Your settings, such as AI difficulty, game speed, sound, and your chosen card back and table.

This information never leaves your device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app removes it. You can also clear your statistics anytime in Settings.

Ratings

Pedro may occasionally show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. That prompt is handled entirely by Apple; we don't receive any information from it.

Children

Pedro is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds online play or purchases, we'll update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
