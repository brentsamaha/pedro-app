Pedro

Louisiana's card game for iPhone and iPad

Support

Questions, a rule that doesn't look right, or an idea for the game? Email hello@cajunpedro.com and we'll get back to you.

When reporting a rules question, it helps to describe the hand: the trump suit, who bid, and the cards played.

How to play

Open the app and tap Learn to Play for a guided practice hand, or Rules for the full Louisiana Pedro rules at any time, including during a game.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Pedro works fully offline and never asks you to sign in.

Can I play with friends online? Not yet. Version 1 is you and your partner against two computer players. Online play is on our list.

Where are my stats? Tap Stats on the home screen. They're stored only on your device.

Privacy

Pedro doesn't collect any personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
